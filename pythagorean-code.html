Example

How we worked it out
  1. 1 · Biorhythm number

    Made from your day and month of birth. It describes your inner nature: temperament, abilities and how your body and nerves react.

  2. 2 · Generational number

    Made from your year of birth. It carries the energy of your generation and what you inherit from your family line.

  3. 3 · Life Path number

    The sum of the first two. It shows the main direction and lesson of your life, and it is the last digit of your code.

How the code is made

Every number is a single digit from 1 to 9. When a sum has two digits, add those digits again until one number is left.

The code is the three numbers in order: biorhythm, generational, Life Path. The first two always add up to the third, so the last digit tells you which Life Path section to read.

Biorhythm = day + month. Generational = year. Life Path = biorhythm + generational.
